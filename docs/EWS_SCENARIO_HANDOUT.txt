SCENARIO THEY WILL WALK THROUGH

It is a wet, stormy afternoon in Melbourne. You are at home in the city (Melbourne CBD)
with your elderly parents. Your young grandchild, Kai (8), is at school at St Kilda
Primary. You have your phone nearby but are not watching it closely - the room is a
little noisy. Suddenly your phone buzzes hard.

Walk through the app the way you would in real life. Take your time, and say out loud
what you are thinking as you go. (The facilitator will start the emergency for you.)

1. A flood warning arrives.
   Your phone vibrates and a full-screen red EMERGENCY alert takes over the screen:
   "Flash flooding emergency along the Yarra River." Look at it and tell us:
   - What is happening, and how serious is it?
   - What is it telling you to do? (e.g. move to higher ground, don't drive through
     floodwater, take your kit, call 000 if trapped)

2. See where the flooding is.
   Tap "See on map". The map opens and centres on the flooding along the Yarra River,
   showing how close it is to your home. Describe what you understand: Is your home in
   danger? How far away is the flooding?

3. Tell your family you are safe.
   Tap the big "I'm Safe" button so your family knows you are okay.

4. Check on your family.
   Go to the Family tab. You will see Mum and Dad are marked safe, and your husband is
   safe at work - but Kai has "Not replied" from school. Open Kai's card and try to reach
   out (Call / Nudge, or mark that you know they are safe).

5. Know what to do.
   Go to the Plans tab and open a plan (for example the flood or fire plan). Read the
   steps and think about whether they help you look after your parents and Kai.

6. Warn a neighbour who has no app.
   Imagine your next-door neighbour does not have this app. Try to share the flood alert
   with them so they know to move to safety too.

7. Make the app easier for you.
   Go to the Settings tab. Try turning on dark mode, larger text, or higher contrast so
   the alert is easier to read. Try setting your preferred language if you can find the
   option.

8. Get interrupted, then come back.
   Your parents call out to you - leave the app for a moment, then open it again. Check
   whether it is easy to pick up where you left off and still see the warning.
